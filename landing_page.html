Is now a good time to invest?
Every day, Sentinel gives you a simple, plain-language read on the Indian economy: add more, hold steady, or play it safe. You see how confident the signal is and what changed since yesterday.
Where should the money go?
Based on today's economic conditions, Sentinel tells you which sectors are favoured right now (Banking, Infrastructure, Consumer stocks) and which to avoid. It also tells you whether to lean toward equity, fixed deposits, gold, or a mix. Updated every time conditions change.

A free snapshot of 50 countries showing whether each economy is growing or slowing, useful context before any investment conversation.
