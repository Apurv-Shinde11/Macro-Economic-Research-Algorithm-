How it works
A quantitative engine first. Language second, and only to explain.

The order matters. Findings are computed before anything is written, so the writing can describe the result but never produce it.

01
A proprietary signal engine
Eleven live signals — flows, volatility, credit, valuations, the yield curve, crude, manufacturing activity and more — are scored and cross-checked by rule. The engine looks for signals that agree and signals that contradict each other, because a conclusion built on independent inputs is worth more than one built on several versions of the same input.
Quantitative · deterministic
02
AI constrained to explain
Once the engine has produced its findings, a language model turns them into readable prose. It cannot introduce a signal, change a score, or reach a conclusion the engine did not reach. If the engine has nothing to say, neither does the writing.
Never originates a finding
03
Personalised by mandate
The same macro read means different things to a conservative mandate and an opportunistic growth mandate. Guidance adapts to the mandate type, risk tolerance, and horizon you set — deterministically, not by asking a language model to improvise — while the underlying findings stay identical and auditable.
