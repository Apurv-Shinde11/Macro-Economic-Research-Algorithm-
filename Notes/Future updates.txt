## Tier 1 — Highest impact, build next

**1. Upgrade the NLP engine with an LLM layer**

Your current NLP is keyword matching — it will miss context, sarcasm, and complex macro language entirely. The upgrade is to pipe your headlines through an LLM (Claude or GPT) to get genuine sentiment, entity extraction, and forward-looking signal detection.

```python
# Instead of keyword counting, call Claude API
# "Given these headlines, rate the macro sentiment
#  for Indian equities on a scale of -1 to +1
#  and identify the dominant macro theme."
```

This single change would dramatically improve regime detection quality since the NLP output feeds everything downstream.

---

**2. Historical backtesting module**

Right now SENTINEL has no way to validate whether its regime calls are accurate. You need a `BacktestEngine` that:
- Replays historical macro data (2015–present)
- Compares SENTINEL's regime calls against actual Nifty returns
- Measures whether the playbook would have outperformed a benchmark

Without this, you cannot credibly tell a PMS client "our signals work." This is the single most important credibility feature for institutional sales.

---

**3. Persistent regime tracking + alert system**

Currently every run is stateless — SENTINEL forgets what it said last time. You need:
- A lightweight database (SQLite locally, Supabase for cloud) to store each run's output
- Regime change detection — alert when regime flips (e.g. `STABLE_GROWTH` → `LIQUIDITY_TIGHTENING`)
- Email or WhatsApp alert when a high-priority trigger fires

```python
# Pseudo-logic
if current_regime != last_stored_regime:
    send_alert(f"Regime shift detected: {last_regime} → {current_regime}")
```

## Tier 2 — Build within 4–6 weeks

**4. FII/DII flow tracker**

FII flows are one of the most powerful leading indicators for Indian equities and your system currently ignores them entirely. NSE publishes daily FII/DII data. A dedicated `FlowEngine` that tracks:
- Daily net FII equity flows
- Rolling 5-day and 20-day flow momentum
- Correlation of flow trend with regime

This feeds directly into `LiquidityEngine` and improves its score quality significantly.

**5. Earnings season overlay**

India has four earnings seasons per year. During earnings, macro signals get noise from individual stock moves. A `EarningsCalendarEngine` that:
- Knows when earnings season is active
- Flags that bottom-up signals should dominate top-down macro signals
- Adjusts conviction scores downward during high-dispersion earnings periods

**6. Multi-asset correlation matrix**

Currently your asset impact outputs are static labels ("Overweight equities"). A `CorrelationEngine` that computes rolling 60-day correlations between Nifty, gold, INR, crude, and US10Y would make the asset impact outputs dynamic and genuinely useful for portfolio construction.

## Tier 3 — When you have paying clients

**7. Client portfolio integration**

Allow a PMS firm to input their current portfolio holdings. SENTINEL then:
- Compares their current allocation against the regime-recommended allocation
- Quantifies the gap ("You are 15% overweight IT vs our recommendation")
- Generates a rebalancing plan with specific trade suggestions

This transforms SENTINEL from a research tool into an actionable portfolio management tool — dramatically increasing willingness to pay.

**8. PDF report generation**

Auto-generate a branded, professional PDF report after every run — the kind a PMS manager can forward directly to their investment committee or clients. Use `reportlab` or `weasyprint`. This is a major perceived value-add for institutional buyers.

**9. Multi-user access with firm-level customisation**

Add Streamlit authentication so multiple analysts at the same firm can log in, with firm-specific parameters (their benchmark, their risk tolerance, their sector universe).
