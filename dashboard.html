How to use Sentinel
Morning Ritual
Check Sentinel each morning before market open. Your regime and conviction level set the tone for the day: position sizing, sector tilts, and risk decisions should all be made in the context of the current macro signal. Don't trade against the regime.
Understanding Confidence
Confidence measures how strongly today's macro data supports the dominant regime. Above 65% means signals are aligned and conviction is reliable. Below 45% means conditions are noisy; monitor but avoid aggressive new positions.
Acting on Conviction
HIGH confidence + ACT: add to your clients' investments now — conditions are strongly supportive. MEDIUM + WAIT: make small changes only; avoid aggressive moves until confidence improves. LOW confidence: hold existing positions, raise cash buffer, do not initiate new risk. Never fight the confidence level.
Reading the Archive
The Historical Archive logs every regime call with its Suggested Action and Outcome. Look at the Suggested Action column across rows. Three consecutive LOW confidence HOLD readings means the market conditions are genuinely unclear. Use it to calibrate your confidence in the current read.
When Conditions Change
A Challenger Regime alert means an alternative macro state is gaining weight in the data. If it crosses 45% confidence, a regime shift may be imminent. Run Sentinel again within 24 hours for a fresh read, and check your alert thresholds in the sidebar.

Welcome to Sentinel
Sentinel is a real-time macro intelligence engine. It reads RBI policy, FII flows, yield curves, and liquidity conditions, then classifies India's current investment environment and tells you what to do with your portfolio today.
What is Sentinel?
A macro reading engine that analyses real-time data and classifies the investment environment; you'll know whether to invest more, stay put, or play it safe.
What is a Regime?
Think of it like a season. A Liquidity Driven Expansion is like summer: a good time to invest. A Rising Rates environment is like monsoon: time to be careful. Sentinel tells you which season you're in.
