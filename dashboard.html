How to use Sentinel
01
Morning Ritual
Check Sentinel each morning before market open. Your regime and conviction level set the tone for the day — position sizing, sector tilts, and risk decisions should all be made in the context of the current macro signal. Don't trade against the regime.
02
Understanding Confidence
Confidence is not a prediction of returns — it measures how strongly today's macro data supports the dominant regime. Above 65% means signals are aligned and conviction is reliable. Below 45% means conditions are noisy; monitor but avoid aggressive new positions.
03
Acting on Conviction
HIGH conviction + ACT — deploy with full sizing into regime-appropriate assets. MEDIUM + WAIT — selective additions only; avoid high-beta bets. LOW conviction — hold existing positions, raise cash buffer, do not initiate new risk. Never fight the conviction level.
04
Reading the Archive
The Historical Archive logs every regime call with its Implied Action and Outcome. Compare action columns across rows — three consecutive LOW-conviction HOLD signals means macro conditions are genuinely unclear. Use it to calibrate your confidence in the current read.
05
When Conditions Change
A Challenger Regime alert means an alternative macro state is gaining weight in the data. If it crosses 45% confidence, a regime shift may be imminent. Run Sentinel again within 24 hours for a fresh read, and check your alert thresholds in the sidebar.

Welcome to Sentinel
Sentinel is a real-time macro intelligence engine. It reads RBI policy, FII flows, yield curves, and liquidity conditions — then classifies India's current investment environment and tells you what to do with your portfolio today.
What is Sentinel?
A macro reading engine that analyses real-time data and classifies the investment climate — so you know whether to deploy, wait, or protect capital.
What is a Regime?
Think of it like a season. A Liquidity Driven Expansion is like summer — deploy capital. A Monetary Tightening is like monsoon — take cover. Sentinel tells you which season you're in.
What do I do first?
